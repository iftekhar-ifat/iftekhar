[Accountant AI](https://getaccountantai.com) is an AI-powered finance platform that automates the tedious parts of bookkeeping — data entry, document processing, transaction categorization, and report generation. Instead of manually keying in every line, users describe a transaction in plain language or upload a document, and the AI converts it into a clean, categorized record. It is built for businesses, freelancers, accountants, and financial advisors who want accountant-grade output without the manual grind.

## Key Features

- **AI Transaction Generator**: Turn natural-language descriptions into structured, categorized transactions.
- **Document to Transaction**: Extract transactions from bank statements, receipts, and CSVs using AI-powered OCR.
- **Invoice Automation**: Generate and manage invoices without manual formatting.
- **AI Assistant**: A chat interface to ask questions about your finances and get instant answers.
- **Transaction Management**: Organize, search, and filter transactions with advanced controls.
- **Project Management**: Group finances by business unit or client to keep books separated.
- **Analytics & Statistics**: Visualize spending and income with calendar-based views.
- **Scheduled Transactions**: Automate recurring entries with reminders.
- **Accountant Reports**: Generate income statements, balance sheets, and cash-flow reports.
- **Data Backup**: Manage profile, settings, and secure backups of your financial data.

## How It Works

1. **Sign Up**: Create an account — no credit card required.
2. **Add Transactions**: Upload documents or type transaction details in plain language.
3. **Let the AI Process**: Transactions are extracted, categorized, and organized automatically.
4. **Review & Report**: Explore analytics, generate accountant reports, and manage everything from the dashboard.
